# MedTrack User Manual

Welcome to **MedTrack**! This application is designed to help you efficiently manage your patient records, track clinic visits, and monitor patient vitals all in one fast, easy-to-use interface.

## Overview of Features

### 🏠 Dashboard

When you open MedTrack, you will first see the **Dashboard**. This is your daily command center where you can view:

- **Quick Stats**: A high-level overview of total registered patients and recent clinic activity.
- **Recent Visits**: A quick list of the patients who have most recently visited the clinic.
- **Follow-ups**: A helpful list of patients who are scheduled or recommended to be seen for a follow-up.

### 🔍 Global Search

At the top of the screen, you will find the **Global Search** bar.

- You can click it or press `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) from anywhere in the app to open it.
- Use this to instantly find any patient by typing their name, phone number, or ID. This is the fastest way to pull up a patient's record during a busy day.

### 👥 Patient Management

Navigate to the **Patients** tab to view a complete, searchable list of all registered patients.

- **Adding a Patient**: Click the "Add Patient" button to register someone new. You will be prompted to enter basic details like Name, Age, Gender, and Contact Information. The system will alert you if you might be adding a duplicate patient.
- **Viewing a Patient**: Click on any patient in the table to open their detailed, individual profile.
- **Managing Profiles**: In the patient list or on their profile, you can easily edit their details, export their information, or remove them from the system.

### 🩺 Visits & Vitals

From a patient's individual profile, you can manage their complete medical history with your clinic.

- **Logging a New Visit**: Click "Add Visit" from the patient's profile. You can record the date, symptoms, diagnosis, and general notes.
- **Recording Vitals**: While adding a visit, you have the option to record vitals for that day (e.g., Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, Temperature, Weight). _Vitals are completely optional._
- **Visit Timeline**: The patient's profile displays a beautiful, chronological timeline of all their past visits. This makes it effortless to review their previous symptoms and treatments before they walk into your office.

## Helpful Tips

- **Speed is Key**: The app is built to minimize typing. Use the `Ctrl + K` search shortcut to jump between patients without navigating through menus.
- **Dark Mode**: If you prefer working with darker screens, the application fully supports Dark Mode to reduce eye strain.
- **Notifications**: If an action fails (for instance, a temporary internet issue preventing a visit from saving), you will receive a user-friendly pop-up notification at the bottom of the screen.
